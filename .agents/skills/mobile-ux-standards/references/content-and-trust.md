# Content and trust

- Use familiar, specific verbs: “Post task”, “Save profile”, “Cancel application”. Avoid generic “Submit” when a clearer outcome exists.
- State prerequisites, permissions, fees, deadlines, payment handling, and consequences before commitment.
- Do not create false urgency, hidden opt-ins, misleading defaults, or success language before confirmation.
- Keep dates, money, phone numbers, and status copy locale-aware and derived from real data.
- Preserve proper names and user-entered casing. Allow long and multilingual content to wrap.
- Permission prompts explain why access is useful, what happens if declined, and the available alternative.
- Help and error copy should be concise, actionable, and free of internal implementation jargon.
